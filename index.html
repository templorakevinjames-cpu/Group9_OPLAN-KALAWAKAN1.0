About the Game

Game Description: Oplan Kalawakan is an action-packed retro space shooter where players navigate through intense celestial battlegrounds to protect our star systems against relentless cosmic invasions.

Game Concept: Combining classic arcade shoot-'em-up mechanics with a vibrant space-faring aesthetic, players pilot customized spacecraft through dynamic boss battles, survival waves, and co-op combat challenges.

🇵🇭 Pinoy Theme Custom Rocket Skins Single & 2-Player Co-Op Boss Battles

Filipino Cultural Theme

  • Cultural Visuals: Dynamic backgrounds styled after Manila Bay sunsets, tropical twilight, and the iconic Three Stars and the Sun.
  • Mythological Bosses: Face legendary cosmic monsters inspired by Philippine folklore, including Spacebalang and the planet-devouring Bakunawa.
  • Iconic Pinoy Ships: Pilot culturally customized spacecraft skins such as the Bakbakan Jeepney, Trike Trooper, Balangay Navigator, and PAL Jet[cite: 1].
